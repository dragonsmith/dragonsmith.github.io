Kirill Kuznetsov

Head of SRE at Evil Martians. I inflict modern Kubernetes infrastructure and common-sense deploys on earthlings.

What I do

I build and run infrastructure for product teams: Kubernetes clusters, CI pipelines, observability, and the deploy processes that make shipping on a Friday merely inadvisable instead of terrifying.

Most of my work is helping teams replace "it works on that one server nobody touches" with something documented, reproducible, and pleasantly dull. Dull infrastructure is my favourite kind.

What I believe

  • There are no money problems. A scary cloud bill is usually a technical limitation nobody has named yet.
  • Heroku, public cloud, and bare metal aren't tiers. They're answers to different questions about your business.
  • A deploy tool is not infrastructure. Somebody still has to build the monitoring, the logs, and the networking.
  • Common sense beats clever. Clever gets paged at 3 a.m.
  • If a process lives only in someone's head, it's not a process yet. It's a rumour.
